How the sheet reads your session

Each day now lists all 6 exercises. The two MAIN lifts (orange bar) drive the week-by-week plan — their rep target changes every block (that's the periodization). The ACC accessories run simple double progression in their own, higher rep range, and CORE moves you just progress by adding time or reps. Every exercise gets its own verdict.

Weights & your dumbbells

Enter weight in pounds, per dumbbell — the kg equivalent shows underneath automatically. The quick buttons match your plates: 22 · 33 · 44 · 55 lb. Because your jumps are a big +11 lb, each ✅ ADD LOAD sends you to the next plate; once you're at 55 lb the app switches you to the overload ladder automatically (tempo → pause → extra set → less rest).

Session logging

Timing starts automatically when you log your first set of a day. Tap Finish exercise ✓ at the bottom of each exercise as you complete it (the card turns green; the day header shows “● in progress · 3/6 exercises done”). When you finish the last exercise of the day, the session is logged automatically — date, start–end time, and duration — to the Session Log below. Tapped one by mistake? Tap it again to undo. Duration is wall-clock based, so it stays accurate even if your screen locks between sets. Tap ⏱ Log up top to export the log as a spreadsheet.

How saving works on your iPad

Your entries save automatically on this device as you type (watch the “Saved ✓” indicator top-right). For it to stick, open this file in Safari: save it to your Files app, open it once in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app, fully offline.

Once a week tap ⬇︎ Backup to save a small file to your Files app. If anything ever resets, tap ⬆︎ Restore and pick it. That backup also moves your log to a new device.
